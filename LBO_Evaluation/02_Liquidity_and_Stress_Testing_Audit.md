### 2.2 In-Depth Analysis of Waterfall Line Items

#### A. Pre-IFRS 16 Normalized Cash EBITDA (`'Debt & Covenants'!F77:L77`)
- **Formula & Logic**: Sourced from `'Active Case'!M30:S30`. Pre-IFRS 16 EBITDA reflects true operational cash flow before store lease payments are capitalized under IFRS 16. It starts at **€10,143.13M in 2026E** and compounds at **5.50% annually** to reach **€15,644.50M in 2032E**.
- **Accounting Reconciliation**: Reported IFRS 16 EBITDA in `'Active Case'!M27` is €12,013.8M in 2026E. The model subtracts cash store lease rental payments (`'Active Case'!M28` = -€1,870.7M) to arrive at Normalized Cash EBITDA (`M30` = €10,143.13M). This ensures that rent is treated as a senior operating charge rather than debt service, aligning with conservative credit rating agency methodologies.

#### B. Cash Corporate Income Tax Paid (`'Debt & Covenants'!F78:L78`)
- **Formula & Logic**: Sourced from `'Tax & WACC'!F22:L22` (`=-MAX(0, F23) * $E$7`). Cash tax payments increase from **-€1,428.26M in 2026E to -€2,004.05M in 2032E**.
- **Interaction with Spanish Tax Legislation (Art. 16.1 LIS)**:
  Under Article 16.1 of the Spanish Corporate Income Tax Act (LIS), net financial expenses are deductible up to a statutory ceiling of **30.0% of Operating EBITDA** (or a €1.0M statutory safe-harbor). Because total BidCo LBO gross interest expenses (including PIK interest and structuring fee amortization) exceed 30% of EBITDA during 2026E–2031E (e.g., in 2026E, subject interest is €5,399.5M vs a 30% cap of €3,042.9M), the excess €2,356.6M is disallowed for tax purposes and added back to taxable income (`'Tax & WACC'!F20`).
  Consequently, Adjusted Consolidated Taxable Income (`F23`) equals exactly `EBIT - 30% EBITDA`. Applying the standard 25.0% Spanish CIT rate yields:
  $$\text{Cash CIT Paid} = 25\% \times (8,755.98 - 3,042.94) = 25\% \times 5,713.04 = €1,428.26\text{M}$$
  This mechanism fully normalizes cash tax payments and creates a historical carryforward reserve of **€8,855.1M in deferred tax assets (DTAs)** that begins unwinding in 2032E.

#### C. Working Capital Cash Inflow (`'Debt & Covenants'!F79:L79`)

### 2.3 Bank DSCR vs Total DSCR Covenant Analysis
In European leveraged finance practice, Senior Facilities Agreements (SFAs) distinguish between **Bank DSCR** (which measures mandatory debt service coverage against non-discretionary operating cash flow) and **Total DSCR** (which evaluates cash flow after all uncommitted capital expenditure).

1. **Bank DSCR (Contractual SFA Definition)**:
   $$\text{Bank DSCR} = \frac{\text{Pre-IFRS 16 Normalized Cash EBITDA} - \text{Cash Taxes} + (-\Delta\text{NOF}) - \text{Maintenance Capex}}{\text{Cash Interest} + \text{Mandatory TLA Scheduled Amortization}}$$
   - **2026E**: **1.80x** vs 1.25x covenant (+55 bps headroom; €2,271.8M cash cushion).
   - **2027E**: **1.66x** vs 1.25x covenant (+41 bps headroom; €2,002.9M cash cushion).
   - **2028E (Cycle Low)**: **1.59x** vs 1.25x covenant (+34 bps headroom; €1,861.3M cash cushion; **26.9% safety cushion**).
   - **2029E**: **1.82x** vs 1.25x covenant (+57 bps headroom; €2,977.1M cash cushion).
   - **2030E**: **1.78x** vs 1.25x covenant (+53 bps headroom; €3,062.9M cash cushion).
   - **2031E**: **2.09x** vs 1.25x covenant (+84 bps headroom; €4,432.9M cash cushion).
   - **2032E**: **3.08x** vs 1.25x covenant (+183 bps headroom; €7,089.5M cash cushion).

2. **Total DSCR (Conservative Post-Expansion Capex Lens)**:
   $$\text{Total DSCR} = \frac{\text{CFADS excl. Exp. Capex} - \text{US Expansion Capex}}{\text{Total Debt Service}}$$
   - **2026E**: **1.62x** (+37 bps cushion / 29.9% headroom).
   - **2027E**: **1.51x** (+26 bps cushion / 20.5% headroom).
   - **2028E (Cycle Low)**: **1.44x** (+19 bps cushion / **15.4% headroom**).
   - **2029E**: **1.66x** (+41 bps cushion / 32.8% headroom).
   - **2030E**: **1.63x** (+38 bps cushion / 30.3% headroom).
   - **2031E**: **1.91x** (+66 bps cushion / 52.8% headroom).
   - **2032E**: **2.83x** (+158 bps cushion / 126.2% headroom).

### 5.2 Committed Revolving Credit Facility (RCF) Dynamics
The financing package includes a **€1,000.0M committed Revolving Credit Facility (RCF)** structured to provide seasonal intra-year working capital flexibility and backstop downside shocks (`'Debt & Covenants'` rows 56 to 62).

1. **Automatic Drawing & Repayment Mechanics**:
   In `'Debt & Covenants'!F58`, the annual net change in RCF is governed by:
   $$=+IF(F87 < 0, \; MIN(\$E\$14 - F57, \; -F87), \; -MIN(F57, \; F87))$$
   - If available excess cash (`F87`) falls below zero (i.e., FCFE is insufficient to maintain the €1,000M cash floor), the model automatically draws from the RCF up to the committed limit of €1,000.0M (`$E$14`).
   - If excess cash is positive, it automatically repays any prior drawn balance before any cash sweep is applied.
2. **Base Case Drawdown Status**:
   In the Base Case, the RCF experiences **€0.00 draws in all 7 years** (`F58:L58 = 0`). The facility remains **100% undrawn and fully available (€1,000.0M unencumbered)** at all times (`MAX(0, $E$14 - F59) = €1,000.0M`).

4. **Total Available Liquidity Buffer**:
   Combining balance sheet cash and the undrawn RCF facility, Inditex’s total immediate liquidity evolves as follows:
   - 2026E: **€2,389.59M** (€1,389.6M cash + €1,000M RCF)

### 7.2 Proof of Model Architecture & Zero Circularity
A critical finding of this audit is that **`Inditex_LBO_Institutional_v4.xlsx` contains zero circular references (`Iterative Calculation = False`)**:

1. **Decoupling of Cash Sweep from TLB Interest**:
   In standard LBO models, cash sweep circularity occurs when the debt sweep depends on cash flow, while cash flow depends on interest expense, which in turn depends on the post-sweep debt balance. In this model, `'Debt & Covenants'!F36` calculates TLB cash interest as:
   $$=-E9 \times \text{AVERAGE}(F29, \; F32)$$
   Where `F29` is BoP TLB and `F32` is the TLB balance *before optional sweep*. Because interest is computed on pre-sweep balances, the sweep does not feed back into the interest charge within the same period, completely breaking circularity while adhering to standard European banking convention (where prepayments occur at fiscal year-end).
2. **Decoupling of Spanish Tax Deductibility from Debt Sizing**:
   In `'Tax & WACC'`, net interest expense subject to limitation (`F29`) exceeds the 30% EBITDA cap (`F28`) in all operational years. The disallowed interest added back in row 20 ensures that taxable income (`F23`) equals `EBIT - 30% EBITDA`. Consequently, cash tax paid (`F22`) depends solely on operating performance, preventing circular feedback between debt interest and tax shields.
3. **Pre-Financing Capex & D&A Vintage Architecture**:
   Depreciation is calculated via a standalone vintage matrix (`'Active Case'` rows 47 to 66) using half-year convention straight-line depreciation over a 10-year asset life. D&A feeds into EBIT independently of the capital structure.

## 8. Institutional Risk Assessment & Recommendations for the Investment Committee

### 8.1 Key Risk Factors Identified
1. **Unhedged Floating Senior Debt**:
   The model currently assumes 100% floating debt on Senior TLA and TLB (€51.9B initial balance). While the structure comfortably absorbs up to +250 bps of rate increases, market syndication standards for European mega-LBOs require mandatory hedging.
   - *Recommendation*: Mandate that BidCo execute **interest rate caps or fixed-rate swaps covering at least 65.0% of Senior Debt for the first 36 months** post-closing, locking in Euribor below 4.00%.
2. **Supplier Working Capital Sensitivity**:
   The €1.90B cumulative non-debt cash flow relies on maintaining an extended DPO of ~200 days (-21.54% of sales). If European macroeconomic distress or supply-chain disruptions cause vendors to demand shorter payment terms (e.g., DPO contracting to 160 days), working capital would temporarily reverse into a cash drain.
   - *Recommendation*: Establish a committed €1.50B Reverse Factoring / Supply Chain Finance facility supported by partner banks, allowing suppliers to discount receivables without draining Inditex’s cash balance.
3. **US Expansion Execution & Tariffs**:
   The business plan allocates €6.0B in cumulative capex for US automated logistics hubs and flagship stores. Potential trade barriers, import tariffs on apparel, or construction cost overruns could compress returns.
   - *Recommendation*: Maintain the contractual flexibility embedded in the SFA where US expansion capex is excluded from the Bank DSCR covenant, preserving sponsor discretion to delay or scale down capital outlays if market conditions deteriorate.
